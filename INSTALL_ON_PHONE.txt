========================================
  INSTALL ON YOUR PHONE (Android/iPhone)
========================================

This app works like an APK - install it directly on your phone!

STEP 1: Start the App on Your Computer
---------------------------------------
Double-click: start-app.bat

STEP 2: Find Your Computer's IP Address
-----------------------------------------
Open Command Prompt and type: ipconfig

Look for "IPv4 Address" - it looks like: 192.168.1.xxx

STEP 3: Connect Your Phone
----------------------------
Make sure your phone is on the SAME WiFi as your computer.

STEP 4: Open Phone Browser
----------------------------
Type in browser: http://192.168.1.xxx:3000
(Replace with your actual IP address)

STEP 5: Install as App
------------------------

For Android (Chrome):
  1. Tap the 3 dots menu (top right)
  2. Tap "Add to Home screen" or "Install app"
  3. Tap "Install"
  4. App appears on your home screen!

For iPhone (Safari):
  1. Tap the Share button (square with arrow)
  2. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen"
  3. Tap "Add"
  4. App appears on your home screen!

========================================
  STORAGE - NO CLOUD NEEDED!
========================================

Your data is stored locally on your computer:

- Analysis data: backend/data/

Everything stays on YOUR machine - no cloud storage required!
